Endless Guided Runner Arcade

You play as a blind runner moving forward with complete trust. Your guide runs right behind you, telling you when to steer LEFT, RIGHT, JUMP over obstacles, or SLIDE under branches!

🎮 How To Play (iPad & Mobile):
• Listen / Read: Follow the guide's audio & text hints!
• Touch Controls: Use the on-screen directional buttons or swipe.
• Keyboard: Arrow keys or A/D to move, Space/W to jump, S to slide.
• Microphone (Optional): Say "Left", "Right", "Jump", "Slide".
